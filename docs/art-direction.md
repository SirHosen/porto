# Midnight Panel — art direction

Ink black (#101010), warm manga paper (#eeeade), and vermilion (#f24b3e). Large condensed titles, italic editorial accents, architectural night atmosphere, restrained parallax, and a faceted three-dimensional capability core.

The original city illustration was created with the built-in image generation tool using the imagegen skill. The delivered website asset is `assets/images/midnight-city.webp`. The generated source was converted to WebP at quality 86 with ImageMagick; its composition was not edited. It is 1672 × 941 pixels. Fonts are bundled with their original licenses.

The project covers are original HTML/CSS concept visuals. The WebGL scene is actual generated mesh geometry, not an image or a video of a 3D object.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original wide website hero background artwork for a developer portfolio titled Midnight Panel, anime and manga cinematic theme. Create a stunning cinematic anime establishing shot of a futuristic Japanese city at midnight seen from a rooftop, richly detailed architectural linework, tall buildings and rooftop antennas receding into atmospheric haze, giant crimson red moon at upper right, dark charcoal black sky, muted ivory moonlit buildings, selective scarlet lighting and a few pale teal windows. Original hand-inked manga meets high-end anime film background, dramatic atmospheric depth, delicate crosshatching, subtle film grain. Composition: wide 16:9 landscape, city concentrated in the lower half and the right, upper left and left middle mostly dark negative space to put large white typography in HTML over it. A tiny solitary silhouette with a windblown coat on a rooftop at lower right for scale. Strong architectural perspective and layered skyline. Refined restrained palette, beautiful dramatic light, not generic neon cyberpunk. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no UI. This is final artwork, not a website mockup.
